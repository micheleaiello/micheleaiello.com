This year I treated myself to a really nice advent calendar; one from [ThePiHut](https://thepihut.com/) (one of my favourite stores).

[![ThePiHut Advent Calednar - Let it Glow!](/assets/images/20260216-let-it-glow.jpg){: .align-center}](https://thepihut.com/products/maker-advent-calendar-let-it-glow-inc-raspberry-pi-pico-h)

For the last couple of years they've been creating these nice packages with hardware and step by step guides on how to build something fun with a Raspberry Pi Pico. The one I got is called [Let it Glow!](https://thepihut.com/products/maker-advent-calendar-let-it-glow-inc-raspberry-pi-pico-h) and I had a lot of fun building it.

Here's a video of the various steps: 

[![Advent Calendar 2026 - Let it Glow!](https://img.youtube.com/vi/_zfeelo4cRA/hqdefault.jpg){: .align-center}](https://youtu.be/_zfeelo4cRA)
